FEATURES

Everything missing in a health app
that gets devs.

🌅

Good morning with context

Opens with the note you left yesterday. You don't start your day from scratch — you pick up where you stopped.

🌙

Honest end of day

Mood in 1 click, optional journal, and a note for tomorrow. 20 seconds to close the day right.

💧

Reminders without being annoying

Hydration, stretching and eye rest at the interval you choose. They vanish when you're idle.

🔥

Care streak

Counter of consecutive days logging. Not to punish you — to show you you're taking care.

☁️

Optional sync

Works 100% offline. When you want, sign in and sync everything across machines via Supabase.

🎨

Actually pretty

Geist + Geist Mono, light and dark theme following the system. Lives quietly in the tray, shows up only when needed.
